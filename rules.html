VortexMC Official Rules

Network-wide guidelines for the VortexMC community.

1. Spamming

Do not spam messages, commands, chat or advertisements.

2. Respect

Disrespectful behavior, harassment, slurs, threats and inappropriate behavior are not permitted.

3. Fair Play

Do not use unfair advantages, cheats, prohibited client modifications, exploits or duplication glitches.

4. Advertising

Advertising, self-promotion, suspicious links and IP-grabbing are not allowed without permission.

5. Staff

Do not disrespect, impersonate or abuse staff members or support systems.

6. Builds & Names

Do not create inappropriate structures or use inappropriate usernames, skins or item names.

7. Punishments

Do not bypass disciplinary actions or publicly abuse/discuss punishments.

8. Scamming & Griefing

Gameplay restrictions may differ by gamemode. Follow the rules displayed for each VortexMC realm.

9. Discord Rules

Keep Discord discussions respectful. No spam, advertising, bypassing filters, suspicious links, bot attacks or support-system abuse.

10. Staff Discretion

Staff may take action against behavior that harms the community even if it is not explicitly listed above.
